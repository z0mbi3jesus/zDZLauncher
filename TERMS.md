## Mods, subscriptions, and joining

Selecting a server queries its advertised requirements. Clicking **Get All Mods**
or **Join Server** requests subscriptions to missing items and downloads or
updates through Steam, using available Workshop IDs. This may use substantial
bandwidth and disk space and adds subscriptions to your Steam account. Steam
may subsequently update subscribed items under its own behavior and terms.

**Get All Mods** prepares the loadout without launching. **Join Server** waits
for required items to be ready, checks the advertised list again, then requests
a BattlEye game launch with the player profile, server, and mod arguments.
Saved mod profiles are separate from the DayZ player profile.

**Cancel** stops the launcher's preparation and prevents that pending automatic
launch. It does not unsubscribe items or guarantee cancellation of downloads
already queued in Steam. Manage subscriptions and queued downloads in Steam.

Servers may omit IDs or advertise incomplete requirements. **Set ID** stores a
user-supplied Workshop mapping; verify the correct item before using it.
Do not use the application to obtain unauthorized access to private content.
No exact server/client mod-version match or complete dependency resolution is
guaranteed. Mods are not supplied or independently redistributed by the Author.

## Player identity and game launch

Player identity is read from the signed-in Steam client. Existing DayZ settings
profiles in the normal Windows Documents DayZ folder can be selected; custom
profile directories are not automatically detected. Existing game settings files
are read without modification by this application. The game, Steam, and
BattlEye may write their own files while running.

A successful launch means that the BattlEye bootstrap process started; it does
not confirm server admission or that a particular survivor, inventory, or progress
was restored. Those outcomes are handled by the game and relevant server.
The application does not provide in-game character editing or recovery.
